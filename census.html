Overview

01 · Program Counts
Unique ODW headcount (Active or Paused) per program, split by role. Role is decided by G-Code (cl-1/2/3 → Lead, pm-1 → Program Manager, everything else → Analyst; Danilo Vuletic is always counted as Program Manager).
02 · i18n Team Counts
International analysts only (English, Leads and Program Managers excluded; Active or Paused), grouped by language from the G-Code prefix, split by level. Defaults to Content Curation — use the dropdown for other programs or the combined view.
03 · ODW vs. Contracts Reconciliation
Headcount rule: Active or Paused counts, in either file. ODW workers vs. contract workers, each deduplicated by ID (a worker can hold more than one contract).
